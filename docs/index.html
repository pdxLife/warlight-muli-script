Tidy Up Your Dashboard

v3.45.0

A userscript for war.app in a desktop browser: a tidier dashboard, tournament tools, community ladders and leagues, and more.

Install
  1. Install Tampermonkey in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. In Chrome or Edge, also turn on Allow User Scripts in Tampermonkey's Details on the extensions page, or the script won't run.
  2. Click Install above and confirm in Tampermonkey.
  3. Open war.app. Updates arrive by themselves.

What it adds How to use it →

Dashboard

Press R to refresh. A game's chat right on the dashboard. Your-turn count in the tab title. Your-turn games sorted by time left before boot. Open Games filter by seats, team games and settings, "Joined" badges, and badges for community event games.

Games

Private notes inside a game, with a note per opponent and who moves first. Search and filters on Past Games. A game's whole chat at once. Players still in the game first in private chat. An optional question before Commit. Each player's pace in a lobby. Chat read aloud.

Tournaments

Search and sort a tournament's players. Bookmark tournaments. A link to the tournament's page on custom.wl-stats.com.

Ladders & community events

AWP World Tour, P/R League, Nations Cup, Clan Cup and more on the Ladders page, with your own place. Standings on Community Events. Your rank on Quickmatch leaderboards.

Profiles

Your private notes, AWP World Tour stats and community tournament stats on a player's profile.

Clans

Clan list search, and a clan's squads in the current community events on its page.

Idle

A check before a market typo spends all your money, confirmations before using powers, time to afford, what a tech is missing and its market cost, the best army camp, better artifact copies, a volume slider and more.

Forum, mail & more

Bookmarks, a formatting toolbar, hiding threads, player search sorted by best match, mail search by player, words and date, block list search, map search. Chat rooms without join/leave notices, with search, mute, @name suggestions, your mentions marked and read-aloud.
