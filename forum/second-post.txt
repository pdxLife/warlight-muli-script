[b]Forum, mail and more[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Bookmarks[/b] in the dashboard sidebar, with quick-bookmark links on threads, tournaments and levels.
[*][b]Forum[/b]: a formatting toolbar, hiding threads, hiding Off-topic and Idle threads, level previews in posts.
[*][b]Mail[/b]: filter by player, by words (subject or first words of the message) and by date.
[*][b]Player Search[/b]: best match first, by level or name, hide level 1–2 accounts, tags for you, AWP ranks and blocked players; paste a profile link to open it.
[*][b]Also[/b]: block list search and count, map search, sortable Common Games with your head-to-head record, total points earned.
[/list]

[b]Settings[/b]
Everything is in your account menu → "Muli's Userscript": every feature has its own switch, grouped by area, with a search box. Export / Import backs up your settings, bookmarks and notes.
[img]https://pdxlife.github.io/warlight-muli-script/media/guide-settings-menu.png[/img]

[b]Privacy and safety[/b]
The script doesn't send your username anywhere. Community stats come from awp.wl-stats.com and custom.wl-stats.com without your war.app login. The features that use Muli's old server (MTL stats and community level records) are off by default.
