[b]Update: what's new since October 14[/b]

The script went from 3.14.5 to 3.37.1 in these five days. If you installed it, you already have it: Tampermonkey updates by itself (the version is in the bottom-right corner of war.app). The first post now lists everything the script does; here is what's new.

[b]New: a full guide[/b] on the website, with a picture for each feature, where to find it and which setting controls it: https://pdxlife.github.io/warlight-muli-script/guide.html

[hr]
[b]In your games[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Notes in a game[/b] (the most-voted Feature Request, "Provide Space for In-Game Notes"): a Notes button next to war.app's Game button, with general notes, which turns you move first on, and a note per player.
[*][b]The whole chat at once[/b]: no more pressing "Load Older Messages" ("Showing more posts in game chat windows").
[*][b]Read chat aloud[/b] ("Text to speech"): a 🔊 on each message, and a setting that reads new messages as they arrive.
[*][b]Private chat list[/b] with the players still in the game first ("Border in PMs").
[*][b]Ask before committing[/b], a setting that's off by default ("Are you sure you want to commit your orders?").
[*][b]Tag players[/b] by typing @ and picking a name.
[/list]

[img]https://pdxlife.github.io/warlight-muli-script/media/guide-game-chat-buttons.png[/img]

[b]Past Games and Open Games[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Past Games filters[/b] ("Search/Sort Functionality for Past Games"): search, in progress / your turn / lobby / finished / out, real-time or multi-day, reverse order.
[*][b]More Open Games filters[/b]: seats from–to, team games only or none, and hiding games by their settings (Commerce, local deployments, cards, fog and more).
[/list]
[img]https://pdxlife.github.io/warlight-muli-script/media/past-games-filter.png[/img]
[img]https://pdxlife.github.io/warlight-muli-script/media/open-games-filter-more.png[/img]

[hr]
[b]Chat rooms[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Hide events[/b]: the "Joined the group" / "Removed from the group due to inactivity" / clan war notices are hidden, so you can read the conversation.
[*][b]Mute[/b] a player without blocking them ("Mute Button"), and a Muted list to undo it.
[*][b]Mentions[/b] of your name are outlined in gold, with "@1" on the tab ("Tagging Players in Public Chat").
[*][b]Search chat[/b], [b]@ tagging[/b] and [b]read aloud[/b].
[*]A [b]Chat button[/b] on the dashboard ("Add Button to Open Chat from MP Dashboard"), and [b]💬 Chat[/b] on each game in My Games: that game's chat opens in a panel on the dashboard, Public and Team, and you can write there too. It shows the last 20 messages; Show all loads the rest.
[/list]
